I'm Jasper Mellor, a 21-year-old self-taught level designer. I build spaces through atmosphere, storytelling and player psychology. I take them from blockout to final art pass myself, covering environment art, lighting, effects, UI, trailers and promotional art.

I've spent the last few years leading small teams and solving the technical problems that come with shipping content to a live audience, from reviving Pandemic Express with an international volunteer team to co-leading a DayZ server project for a community of 100,000+ members.

Most recently I created Final Call, a physics-based bar brawler made in three weeks for an s&box game jam.
